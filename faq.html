Featured How do conversation limits and Meta WhatsApp fees work?

↓

Direct Answer: Each Agentiq tier includes a set monthly quota of AI-handled conversations (e.g. 1,000 for Starter, 2,000 for Growth, 5,000 for Pro). If you exceed your plan's included limit, extra conversations are billed at a flat rate of ₹2.50 each.

Official Meta WhatsApp conversation fees (marketing, utility, service) are charged directly by Meta via your linked payment method and are separate from your Agentiq subscription. All prices shown are exclusive of 18% GST.
