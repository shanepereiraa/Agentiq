Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with AgentIQ as your WhatsApp chatbot agency

What does a WhatsApp chatbot agency do versus self-serve software? +
Self-serve platforms (like WATI, AiSensy, or Interakt) provide raw tools that you must configure, build, and troubleshoot yourself using complex flowchart builders. A done-for-you WhatsApp chatbot agency like AgentIQ handles everything: Meta verification, knowledge base ingestion, custom LLM prompt engineering, POS/CRM integration, human handoff triggers, and ongoing accuracy audits.
How much does a WhatsApp chatbot service cost in India? +
AgentIQ's transparent agency pricing includes a one-time onboarding & architecture setup fee (from ₹24,999) plus a monthly managed retainer (from ₹7,999/mo). This covers infrastructure maintenance, prompt engineering revisions, API uptime, model token costs, and dedicated technical support. Meta WhatsApp conversation utility/marketing charges are billed separately at cost directly by Meta.
How fast can AgentIQ deploy our WhatsApp AI chatbot? +
Our full turnaround time is 7 business days from kickoff. We ingest your menus, service lists, FAQs, and booking rules, configure Meta Cloud API credentials, run real-world simulated test conversations, and launch seamlessly with zero downtime.
Can the chatbot speak fluent Hinglish and Hindi? +
Yes. AgentIQ's assistants are engineered with Indian code-switching prompts powered by Anthropic Claude Haiku and Gemini models, allowing customers to switch freely between pure English, Hindi, and colloquial Hinglish (e.g. "table book karna hai 8 baje").
What happens if a customer asks a query the AI cannot answer? +
Our bots include built-in fallback guardrails. When sentiment is negative, high-value, or unresolvable by the AI, the conversation is instantly flagged and handed off to your team with full WhatsApp notification and chat history.
