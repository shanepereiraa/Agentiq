Frequently Asked Questions

Can AgentIQ handle cancellations and rescheduling autonomously?

Yes. Customers can reply "Reschedule" or "Cancel" on WhatsApp. The bot immediately frees up the old calendar slot and prompts the client to pick a new open time.

How does AgentIQ sync with Petpooja or Google Calendar?

We connect directly via secure OAuth and REST webhooks. As soon as a booking is confirmed in chat or voice, it displays on your POS screen or staff calendar within 500ms.

Can the bot collect an advance token payment before confirming?

Yes. You can require a custom deposit (e.g., ₹500 table reservation fee or consultation charge). The slot is held tentatively and confirmed once UPI payment succeeds.
