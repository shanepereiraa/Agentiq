Frequently Asked Questions

How does this simulator differ from a live production bot?

This browser-based simulator provides an instant preview of prompt responses and conversational persona behavior. In production, AgentIQ connects your bot directly to the Meta Cloud API on your verified WhatsApp Business phone number, linking with your live POS (Petpooja), CRM, or Shopify store for real-time inventory checks, payment links, and booking management.

Can AgentIQ's production chatbots communicate in Hindi and Hinglish?

Yes. Production AgentIQ bots understand and reply natively in English, Hindi, and colloquial Hinglish (e.g. “table book karna hai”, “kal subah appointment mil sakti hai?”). The bots detect the customer's language automatically and match their conversational style with zero lag.

How long does it take to deploy a custom bot for my business?

AgentIQ operates as a 100% done-for-you agency. We handle WhatsApp Business API verification, catalog syncing, conversational prompt engineering, and staff training. Your custom AI assistant is live and taking customer inquiries within 7 business days.

What happens when a customer query requires human intervention?

AgentIQ bots feature intelligent handover triggers. If an inquiry involves complex negotiations, VIP requests, or complaints, the AI pauses and instantly routes the chat to your staff's WhatsApp or team dashboard with full context.
