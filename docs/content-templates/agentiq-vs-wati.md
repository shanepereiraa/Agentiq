## H1

# AgentIQ vs WATI: Done-for-You AI Chatbot or Self-Serve WhatsApp Platform?

## Intro

WATI is primarily a self-serve WhatsApp Business API platform — the business owner or their team logs in and builds automation flows using WATI's visual builder, across Growth / Pro / Business subscription tiers plus a ₹999 one-time pay-as-you-go entry option. WATI also sells paid add-on services (Dedicated Onboarding, Technical Account Management, Professional Services) for teams that want more hand-holding than the self-serve default — so "self-serve" is the base model, not the only option. *(Source: wati.io/pricing, checked 2026-08-09.)* AgentIQ is a done-for-you AI chatbot and voice agent agency: instead of a dashboard to configure, AgentIQ's team designs, trains, deploys, and optimises the WhatsApp assistant for you. This is the same framing AgentIQ already uses on its own homepage FAQ, so it's a safe, pre-verified starting point for this comparison.

## When WATI Might Be the Better Fit

Based on verified facts above: teams with an in-house person who wants to build and iterate on WhatsApp/Instagram/Messenger flows themselves in a visual builder, and who are comfortable paying WATI's quoted rate for Dedicated Onboarding or Professional Services only if/when they decide they need extra help — rather than getting a fully managed build from day one.

## When AgentIQ Is the Better Fit

- You want a working assistant without learning a builder or logging into a dashboard
- You need WhatsApp + Instagram + Website + Voice unified under one assistant
- You want ongoing optimisation handled by a team, not self-managed
- You're an Indian restaurant, salon, clinic, or D2C brand — AgentIQ's core verticals
