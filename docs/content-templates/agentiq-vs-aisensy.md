## H1

# AgentIQ vs AiSensy: Done-for-You AI Chatbot or Self-Serve WhatsApp Platform?

## Intro

AiSensy is a self-serve WhatsApp Business API platform — signup and "Buy Now" self-checkout flows are the primary path, with two core plans (a Drag & Drop Chatbot Builder and a WhatsApp AI Agent Builder) rather than a managed onboarding tier. Sales chat support exists ("Need help choosing? Talk to our team") but no dedicated managed-onboarding product was found on the pricing page. *(Source: aisensy.com/pricing, checked 2026-08-09.)* AgentIQ is a done-for-you AI chatbot and voice agent agency — AgentIQ's team designs, trains, deploys, and optimises the WhatsApp (and Instagram, website, voice) assistant for you, rather than handing you a builder to configure yourself.

## When AiSensy Might Be the Better Fit

Based on verified pricing: budget-conscious businesses that want a self-serve WhatsApp chatbot or AI agent starting around ₹2,500–3,500/month (well below AgentIQ's managed-service starting price) and have someone in-house willing to configure the builder, manage broadcast campaigns, and handle per-message costs directly.

## When AgentIQ Is the Better Fit

- You want a working assistant without learning a builder or logging into a dashboard
- You need WhatsApp + Instagram + Website + Voice unified under one assistant
- You want ongoing optimisation handled by a team, not self-managed
- You're an Indian restaurant, salon, clinic, or D2C brand — AgentIQ's core verticals

## FAQ

**Is AgentIQ more expensive than AiSensy?**
Yes, on sticker price — AiSensy's self-serve plans start around ₹2,500–3,500/month with no setup fee (aisensy.com/pricing, checked 2026-08-09), while AgentIQ is a managed service with a setup fee plus monthly cost. The difference reflects what's included: with AiSensy you configure and manage the chatbot yourself; with AgentIQ a team builds, trains, and optimises it for you. Frame this as a cost-vs-time/expertise trade-off, not a "which is cheaper" claim in isolation.
