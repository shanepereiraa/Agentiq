If you have an in-house team member comfortable building chatbot flows, setting up Meta WhatsApp API broadcast templates, and managing per-message credits directly, AiSensy's self-serve subscription starting around ₹1,500–₹3,500/month offers an accessible entry point.

Questions, answered

Is AgentIQ more expensive than AiSensy?

On sticker price, yes — AiSensy's self-serve plans start around ₹1,500 to ₹3,500/month with self-checkout, while AgentIQ is a fully managed service starting at ₹24,999 setup plus ₹6,999/month. The difference reflects what is included: with AiSensy you configure and manage the chatbot flows yourself; with AgentIQ a team builds, trains, and optimises the assistant for you.

Can I migrate my existing AiSensy flows to AgentIQ?

Yes. AgentIQ's onboarding team collects your existing FAQs, bot flows, catalog, and booking logic during the kickoff call, so you don't need to rebuild anything yourself when switching to a managed service.

Does AiSensy offer AI voice agents?

AiSensy focuses on WhatsApp messaging automation and chatbot workflows. No standalone AI phone or IVR voice agent product is listed on their pricing page. AgentIQ offers 24/7 AI Voice Agents that answer regular inbound phone calls in Hindi and English alongside text chatbots.

How does AgentIQ differ from self-serve platforms like AiSensy?

AiSensy is a self-serve platform where the business owner configures bot builders and broadcast campaigns themselves. AgentIQ is a managed service — our team handles the complete build, AI prompt training, platform deployment, and ongoing optimization. Clients do not log in to configure anything.
