Questions, answered

Is AgentIQ more expensive than Interakt?

On sticker price, yes — Interakt's self-serve plans start around ₹3,499/quarter (Starter) to ₹7,699/quarter (Growth), while AgentIQ is a managed service starting at ₹24,999 setup plus ₹6,999/month. The difference reflects what is included: with Interakt you build and manage the chatbot flows and inbox rules yourself; with AgentIQ a dedicated team builds, trains, and optimises the assistant for you.

Can I migrate my existing Interakt setup to AgentIQ?

Yes. AgentIQ's onboarding team handles the migration by taking your existing templates, FAQs, catalog items, and lead routing rules during the kickoff call.

Does Interakt offer AI voice agents?

Interakt focuses on WhatsApp Business API and Instagram messaging. They do not offer inbound phone-line AI Voice Agents. AgentIQ provides 24/7 AI Voice Agents that handle phone calls in Hindi and English alongside text chatbots.

How does AgentIQ differ from self-serve platforms like Interakt?

Interakt is a self-serve platform providing a shared inbox, broadcast tool, and visual bot builder for your in-house team. AgentIQ is a fully managed service — our team designs, trains, deploys, and continuously tunes your AI assistant so you don't have to manage any platform yourself.
