If you have an in-house person who wants hands-on control over WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger automation flows — and you're comfortable paying extra for WATI's Dedicated Onboarding or Professional Services only if and when you decide you need more support — their self-serve model gives you that flexibility from day one.

Questions, answered

Is AgentIQ more expensive than WATI?

On sticker price, likely yes — WATI's pay-as-you-go entry starts at ₹999 one-time, well below AgentIQ's managed-service pricing. WATI does not publish exact monthly figures for its Growth/Pro/Business tiers, so a precise like-for-like comparison isn't possible from public information. The difference reflects what's included: with WATI you build and manage the bot yourself (or pay extra for their Dedicated Onboarding add-on); with AgentIQ a team builds, trains, and optimises it for you as standard.

Can I migrate my existing WATI flows to AgentIQ?

AgentIQ's onboarding process collects your existing FAQs, catalog, and booking policies during the kickoff call regardless of what platform you're currently using — confirm your specific migration scope directly with the team before signing up.

Does WATI offer AI voice agents?

WATI lists a "WhatsApp Business Calling" feature — a voice channel built inside WhatsApp — on its pricing page. It is not confirmed whether this is AI-automated or a live-agent calling channel, so it should not be assumed equivalent to a standalone AI phone/IVR voice agent. AgentIQ's voice agent product answers regular inbound phone-line calls (not WhatsApp-only) in Hindi and English, 24/7.
