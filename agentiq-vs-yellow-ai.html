Questions, answered

How does AgentIQ's target audience differ from Yellow.ai?

Yellow.ai is built for large enterprises requiring custom multi-channel deployments across 35+ messaging networks and complex IT integrations. AgentIQ is specifically built for growing Indian SMBs (clinics, restaurants, salons, D2C brands) that want a quick, done-for-you AI solution without long enterprise procurement cycles.

Is AgentIQ faster to deploy than Yellow.ai?

Yes. AgentIQ deploys a fully trained custom AI assistant on your WhatsApp, website, or phone line in ~7 days. Enterprise platforms like Yellow.ai typically involve longer integration and implementation lifecycles.

Does Yellow.ai offer voice automation?

Yes. Yellow.ai provides enterprise VoiceX AI for call centers. AgentIQ provides managed 24/7 AI Voice Agents tailored specifically for Indian local phone calls in Hindi and English, integrated directly into SMB workflows.

Is Yellow.ai expensive compared to AgentIQ?

Yellow.ai offers a limited Freemium plan, while its Enterprise plan is custom-quoted based on large-scale message volume and enterprise support tiers. AgentIQ offers clear, fixed pricing for Indian SMBs (₹7,999/month + ₹24,999 one-time setup) with full managed support included.
