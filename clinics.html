Frequently Asked Questions

Can the AI voice agent answer patient phone calls after clinic hours?

Yes. When patients call after 8 PM or on Sundays, AgentIQ's AI voice receptionist picks up instantly, answers common clinic FAQs (timings, doctor availability, location), and books appointments directly onto your calendar or CRM.

Does AgentIQ integrate with Practo or our clinic management software?

Yes. AgentIQ syncs with Practo, Google Calendar, and clinic management software via webhooks and REST APIs. Bookings, patient contact details, and appointment slots update automatically with zero manual entry.

How does the system handle medical emergencies or urgent cases?

The AI is strictly trained with medical triage guardrails. It never provides medical diagnoses or prescriptions. If a caller describes emergency symptoms, the system immediately instructs them to visit the nearest emergency room and triggers an instant alert to on-duty staff.

Can the assistant send automated appointment reminders to reduce patient no-shows?

Yes. AgentIQ sends automated 24-hour and 2-hour WhatsApp appointment reminders with one-tap Confirm or Reschedule buttons, cutting clinic no-show rates by up to 60%.
