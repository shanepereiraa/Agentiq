I'll build you an AI voice agent that answers your business phone 24/7 — in Hindi and English — greets callers naturally, answers questions, and books appointments straight into your calendar.

## FAQ (paste-ready)

**1. Will callers know it's an AI?**
It answers naturally in Hindi and English with a custom greeting — not a press-1-for-this menu. Most callers just get their question answered or their appointment booked. It can transfer to a human whenever needed.

**2. Do I need special phone equipment?**
No. The agent runs on a cloud phone number (Vapi + Twilio). I set the whole thing up and connect it to a number for your business — you don't install anything.

**3. Are there ongoing costs?**
The build is one-time. Live calls use Vapi + telephony minutes billed to your own account (typically a few rupees per minute). I'll set those accounts up with you. Optional: my Monthly Voice Care subscription to manage and tune it for you.

**4. What do you need from me to start?**
Your business details, the questions callers usually ask, your booking process/calendar, and your preferred greeting. I'll send a short checklist right after you order.

**5. Can it book appointments into my existing calendar/CRM?**
Yes — Standard and Premium book appointments and log every call to your Google Sheet or CRM, plus send you a monthly summary of what callers asked.

**6. Are you established on Fiverr?**
I'm newer here and building my portfolio, which is why launch pricing is available now. Message me first and I'll send a free sample call so you can hear exactly how your agent would sound before you buy.
