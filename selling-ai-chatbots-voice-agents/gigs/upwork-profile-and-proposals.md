## 5. Proposal templates

> Rules for all proposals:
> - First 2 lines are everything (that's all the client sees in the list). Lead with THEIR problem, not your bio.
> - Reference a specific detail from their job post — proves you read it.
> - One clear next step. Offer the free personalized demo.
> - Keep it short. Walls of text lose.
> - Never mention AgentIQ, Fiverr, or off-Upwork contact/payment.

---

Hi [Name],

You mentioned [specific detail from post — e.g. "customers asking the same questions on WhatsApp all day"]. That's exactly the kind of thing I automate — an AI chatbot trained on your business that answers instantly, captures the lead, and hands off to you only when it actually needs a human.

For [their business type], I'd build:
• A bot trained on your FAQs, services & tone (Hindi + English if useful)
• Lead capture straight into a Google Sheet or your CRM
• [One tailored item from their post — e.g. "a booking link so people can reserve a table without waiting"]

You'd just answer a few quick questions; I build, you approve, I deploy. If the delivered bot doesn't do what we agree, you don't pay.

Want me to send a free 2-min demo showing how your bot would answer your real customers? Happy to record one for [their business name] before you decide.

— Shane

## 6. First-30-days play on Upwork

1. **Set profile to the copy above; add 1-2 portfolio pieces** — clearly-labeled demo bots you build for common niches (a restaurant FAQ bot, a clinic booking voice agent). Real demos, not fake client work.
2. **Send 10-15 tailored proposals/day** using the templates — only to jobs that fit the ICP (skip generic SaaS/enterprise unless it's a clean fit). Personalize the first two lines every time.
3. **Offer the free personalized demo in every proposal** — it's the conversion lever and the thing that beats reviewed competitors.
4. **Win the first 2-3 jobs even at the low fixed anchors** to bank reviews and Job Success Score. Deliver hard, over-communicate, hit the guarantee.
5. **At handover, pitch the ongoing maintenance contract** ($80-150/mo) as a native Upwork retainer — the recurring revenue bridge.
6. Once reviews exist on Upwork AND Fiverr's gigs are seeded, both channels compound; only then consider adding a third channel.
