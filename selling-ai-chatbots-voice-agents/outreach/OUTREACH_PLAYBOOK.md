## 2. The High-Converting "Mystery Shopper" Methodology

### Step 1: Prospect Selection (Google Maps & Instagram)
1. Search Google Maps for your target city:
   - *"Dental clinics in Bandra / South Mumbai"*
   - *"Aesthetic skin clinic in Indiranagar Bangalore"*
   - *"Fine dining restaurants in CyberCity Gurgaon"*
   - *"D2C Shopify apparel brands India"*
2. Collect:
   - Business Name
   - Owner / Clinic Director Name
   - WhatsApp Number or Instagram Profile
   - Official Email

### Step 2: The 8:45 PM Pattern Interrupt Test
- Send a WhatsApp message or dial their reception line at **8:45 PM** (after regular business hours).
- Inquire: *"Hi, do you have any appointments available tomorrow after 5 PM?"*
- Take a screenshot of the unread message or unanswered call the next morning.

### Step 3: Dispatch Sequence
- **Touch 1 (Next day 10:30 AM):** Send the Mystery Shopper observation highlighting the after-hours revenue gap.
- **Touch 2 (+48 hours):** Send the live demo link ([agentiq.co.in/try-your-bot](https://agentiq.co.in/try-your-bot) or [agentiq.co.in/ai-voice-agents-india](https://agentiq.co.in/ai-voice-agents-india)).
- **Touch 3 (+96 hours):** Breakup & risk-reversal (30-day money-back guarantee).
