Frequently Asked Questions

How do conversation limits and Meta WhatsApp fees work? ↓

Each Agentiq tier includes a set monthly quota of AI-handled conversations. If you exceed your plan's included limit, extra conversations are billed at a flat rate of ₹2.50 each. Official Meta WhatsApp conversation fees (marketing, utility, service) are charged directly by Meta via your linked payment method and are separate from your Agentiq subscription.

What is included in the one-time setup fee? ↓

We handle 100% of the heavy lifting: Meta WhatsApp Business API registration and verification, custom system prompt engineering, knowledge base training on your exact catalogue/menu/services, webhook integrations to your Google Sheets or CRM, and thorough end-to-end testing before going live.

Can I upgrade or cancel my plan anytime? ↓

Yes. All monthly retainers are billed on a flexible month-to-month basis with zero lock-in contracts. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel whenever your business needs change.
