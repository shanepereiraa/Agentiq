Questions, answered

How much does a managed WhatsApp AI chatbot cost in India?

Direct Answer: Managed WhatsApp AI chatbots in India from AgentIQ cost ₹7,999/month plus ₹24,999 one-time setup for the Starter plan (up to 1,000 conversations/mo). The Growth plan is ₹14,999/mo + ₹39,999 setup (up to 2,000 conversations/mo), and the Pro plan is ₹24,999/mo + ₹79,999 setup (up to 5,000 conversations/mo). Every plan includes complete done-for-you design, content training, deployment, and ongoing optimization, backed by a 30-day money-back guarantee.

How long does it take to deploy an AI chatbot for an Indian business?

Direct Answer: AgentIQ deploys custom AI chatbots in a 7-day managed deployment timeline. Following a 20-minute kickoff call, AgentIQ ingests your menu or service catalog, FAQs, and booking policies, trains the model, and launches across WhatsApp, Instagram DM, and website chat with zero coding required from your team.

Which messaging channels does AgentIQ's AI chatbot support?

Direct Answer: AgentIQ powers official WhatsApp Business API, Instagram Direct Messages, and website chat widgets simultaneously. A single trained AI assistant operates across all three channels from one unified backend, connecting to Google Sheets, CRMs, Razorpay/UPI payment links, and Shopify.

Does the chatbot support Hindi and human handoff?

Direct Answer: Yes, AgentIQ AI chatbots reply fluently in English, Hindi, and Hinglish. Whenever a customer requests a human agent or a query requires staff intervention, the bot executes a seamless handoff to your team via instant WhatsApp or email notifications.

What is the difference between WATI, AiSensy, Interakt, and AgentIQ?

Direct Answer: WATI, AiSensy, and Interakt are self-serve software platforms requiring business owners to build, configure, and maintain their own chatbot workflows. AgentIQ is a 100% managed AI chatbot agency in Mumbai that designs, trains, deploys, and updates your AI chatbot and voice agent for you, requiring zero technical effort from your team.
